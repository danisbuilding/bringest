Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Bringest is a sign-up list for potlucks and events. A host writes down what’s needed, shares one link, and guests claim items and leave notes.

There are no accounts. You never give us an email address or a password, and we never send you email. This policy explains the little we do collect, how we use it, who helps us process it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Bringest is operated by LVT8 Technical LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company doing business as DIB Works (“DIB Works,” “we,” “us”). Our address is 1209 Mountain Road PL NE, Ste N, Albuquerque, NM 87110.

To contact us about privacy, email support@dib.works.

There are no accounts

Most services identify you by an account. Bringest identifies a host by a link.

When you start an event we generate two links:

  • a host link, which is secret, and
  • a guest link, which is meant to be shared.

Anyone holding the host link can edit and delete everything in that event. Anyone holding the guest link can add, change or remove any entry on the list, including entries other people made.

We can’t tell these people apart, because there is nothing to sign in to. We don’t know who you are, we can’t verify that a name on a list belongs to the person who typed it, and we can’t recover a lost host link. If you lose it, that event is unreachable — by you and by us.

What we collect

What a host writes

  • The event’s title, the when and where lines, and any description.
  • The items needed, how many of each, and any note you add to an item.
  • An optional exact date and time, used only to build a calendar file.
  • An optional header photo.
  • A colour theme.

What a guest writes

  • A name. It is typed freely and we don’t verify it — it can be a nickname, a first name, or anything at all.
  • The dish, whether it’s hot or cold, how many it serves, and how many of the item it covers.
  • A note for everyone, which appears on the list.
  • A note for the host, which doesn’t. See “Notes for the host” below.

Other people’s information

What you write may include details about other people — a name, a dish someone else is bringing, an allergy mentioned in a note. Only write what you have a reason to share.

Please don’t put sensitive information on a list. That includes health records, financial details and identity documents. We don’t ask for any of it, and a event list is the wrong place for it. If you need to tell a host something private about a medical need, tell them directly instead.

Photos

A header photo is cropped and shrunk in your browser before it’s uploaded, so we receive a 1200×600 image and not the original file. It’s stored with a random, unguessable filename. Anyone who has that image’s address can view it, but it is not listed anywhere and we don’t index it.

Technical data

  • Your IP address, used to count writes per minute so one person can’t flood the service. It’s held in a counter and deleted automatically within the hour. Nothing links it to an event.
  • Standard server logs kept by Cloudflare, our hosting provider.
  • Cookie-free analytics about how pages are used. See “Analytics” below.

What we never collect

  • Email addresses, passwords, phone numbers.
  • Payment details. Bringest is free.
  • Anything read by an AI model. Bringest doesn’t use AI, and nothing you write is sent to an AI provider or used to train a model.

Notes for the host

A guest can leave two kinds of note. The “note for everyone” is shown on the list. The “note for the host” is not — it is left out of every response the guest page receives, not merely hidden on screen, so it never reaches a guest’s browser.

Anyone holding the host link can read every note for the host. If a host forwards their host link to someone, that person can read them too.

How we use it

  • To show the list, and to keep the counts correct when several people sign up at once.
  • To show a host their event, and the notes left for them.
  • To build a calendar file, if a date is set.
  • To store and serve a header photo, if one is added.
  • To understand how Bringest is used, and to improve it.
  • To fix problems, prevent abuse and keep the service running.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We don’t show ads, sell your personal data or build marketing profiles. We have no mailing list and send no marketing email.

We never send email

The “Email” button on your host link doesn’t send anything from us. It opens your own email app with a message already written, addressed to whoever you choose. We never see the address and no email is sent from our servers.

Who we share it with

Service providers. These companies process data for us to run Bringest:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, the database, image storage, network security and analytics. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Cloudflare is the only one.

Anyone with your guest link. They can see everything on the list except the notes for the host — including the names and public notes of everyone who has signed up. A guest link can be forwarded by anyone who has it.

Anyone with a host link. Full access to that event, including every note for the host.

Legal requests. We share data when the law, a court order or a government authority requires it. We may also share it to protect people’s safety or the security of Bringest.

Business changes. If LVT8 Technical LLC, DIB Works or Bringest is merged, sold or reorganized, your data may move to the new owner. This policy keeps applying until you’re told otherwise.

We don’t sell your personal data or share it for targeted advertising.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how Bringest is used — how many pages are opened, which pages, roughly what country visitors are in, what kind of device and browser they use, and how quickly pages load. This tells us whether the service works, not who you are.

It sets no cookies, isn’t an advertising product, and we never send it the contents of a list — no names, no dishes, no notes. Cloudflare already hosts Bringest, so this adds nobody new to the list above. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.

One thing worth knowing: analytics records the address of the page being viewed, and a host page’s address contains the host link. That data stays in our own Cloudflare account, but it does mean a host link can appear in our analytics.

What your browser stores

Bringest keeps two things on your own device, not on our servers:

  • Your host link, so you can get back to your events on that device without hunting for the link. This is the secret that controls your events — on a shared or public computer, clear your browser data when you’re done.
  • The name you last typed, so you don’t retype it every time you sign up for something.

Both stay in your browser. Clearing your site data removes them — including your host link, so save that link somewhere else first.

Where your data is processed

Bringest runs on Cloudflare’s network. Pages and images are served from the location nearest you, and the database has its primary location in the United States. If you use Bringest from another country, your data is transferred to the US.

Security, and who can see your data

Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest by Cloudflare.

It is not end-to-end encrypted. Our systems have to read a list to show it, and a small number of authorized people at DIB Works can technically access the database. We do that to:

  • operate the service, for example to fix a problem someone reports;
  • investigate abuse or a security issue;
  • comply with the law.

Because there are no accounts, the security of an event rests entirely on who holds its links. Treat a host link like a password.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we’ll notify you as the law requires — though without an email address, the only way we can reach you is a notice on the site.

How long we keep it

Events are deleted after three years with no activity. Any change to a event — adding or editing an item, someone signing up, a note, a photo — restarts the three years. Deleting an event removes its items, its sign-ups, its notes and its photo.

When you delete it yourself. A host can delete an event at any time from the host page. It goes immediately.

Rate-limiting counters holding IP addresses are deleted automatically within the hour.

Backups and logs. Cloudflare keeps backups and logs under its own retention policies. Copies may remain for a limited period after something is deleted.

Your choices and rights

On the list itself, a guest can edit or remove their own entry at any time, and a host can edit or remove anything.

Because we have no accounts, we can’t look you up by name or email — an event can only be identified by its link. To ask for a copy of what an event holds, a correction, or deletion, email support@dib.works and include the host link, which is the only way we can tell you’re entitled to it. We’ll respond within 30 days.

If you’re a guest and want your entry removed but don’t have the host link, you can remove it yourself from the list, or ask the host.

Depending on where you live you may have more rights, such as the right to appeal a decision we make on your request. We’ll honor them as the law requires.

Age

Bringest is for people 18 and older. It isn’t directed to children, and we don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

A family event may well include children, and a child might type their name on a list. Hosts: please don’t ask for anything about a child beyond a first name, and don’t put a child’s details in a note.

Changes to this policy

When we update this policy, we’ll post the new version here and change the date at the top. Because we have no way to email you, significant changes will be announced in the app.
