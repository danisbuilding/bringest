Cookie Statement

Effective November 24, 2026

Bringest doesn’t set cookies. There’s no sign-in, so there’s no session to keep, and our analytics doesn’t use them either. That means no cookie banner, because there’s nothing to consent to.

Two things are kept in your browser’s local storage, which isn’t a cookie and isn’t sent with network requests. Everything else about your data is in the Privacy Policy.

What your browser stores

What Name Why How long
Your host link bringest_host_key So you return to your events on this device without hunting for the link Until you clear site data
The name you last typed bringest_name So you don’t retype it every time you sign up for a dish Until you clear site data

Both stay on your device. We can’t read them and they’re never sent to us.

Two things worth knowing:

  • Your host link is the secret that controls your events. Keeping it here is a convenience, not a security measure. On a shared computer, clear your browser data when you’re finished.
  • Clearing site data deletes your host link from this device. If it’s the only copy you have, that event becomes unreachable — by you and by us. Save the link somewhere else first; the host page can put it in an email draft or a calendar entry for you.

You can remove both through your browser’s settings for this site. Bringest keeps working — you’ll retype your name, and you’ll need your host link again.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see whether Bringest works: how many pages are opened, which ones, roughly what country visitors are in, what browser and device they use, and how fast pages load.

It measures page views with a small script and the browser’s own Performance API. It sets no cookie, stores nothing on your device, and gives you no identifier. Cloudflare already hosts Bringest, so this adds nobody new to the companies handling your data. Details: Cloudflare Web Analytics.

We don’t use advertising or retargeting cookies, tracking pixels, session recording, heatmaps, or third-party scripts and fonts loaded from anyone else’s domain.

Changes

If we ever add anything that sets a cookie, we’ll update this page, change the date at the top, and ask for consent where the law requires it.
